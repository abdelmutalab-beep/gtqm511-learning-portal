Understanding Work Teams and Quality Teams

Postgraduate Diploma in Total Quality Management • Student Study Guide & Team Charter Worksheet

Learning Outcomes

  1. Distinguish work groups and work teams.
  2. Identify five types of work teams.
  3. Evaluate team effectiveness through context, composition and processes.
  4. Draft a quality improvement team charter with measurable results.
  5. Apply team design to a workplace quality case.

Part A • Study Guide

Work group vs. work team

A group mainly exchanges information for individual tasks. A team produces shared outputs using complementary skills, positive synergy and mutual accountability.

Problem-solving team

Investigates work problems and recommends better processes, such as improved handover checks.

Self-managed work team

Coordinates much of its own work and monitoring within agreed quality and safety requirements.

Cross-functional team

Unites departments across one end-to-end process, such as patient discharge.

Virtual team

Collaborates across locations through digital tools, e.g. multisite audit reviews.

Multiteam system

Coordinates several interdependent teams toward a larger quality objective.

Context & composition

Resources, supportive leadership, trust and rewards; plus skills, role mix, diversity and size.

Team processes

Common purpose, specific goals, shared process understanding, constructive conflict, efficacy and visible ownership.

Part B • Hospital Discharge Case

A hospital reports long delays after patients are clinically cleared. Discharge requires coordinated ward, pharmacy, billing and transport activities, but departments work toward different priorities.

  1. Explain why the case calls for a team rather than separate departmental actions.
  2. Select the team type and key members.
  3. Name a sponsor, process owner, and specific quality target.
  4. Define outcome, process and balancing metrics.
